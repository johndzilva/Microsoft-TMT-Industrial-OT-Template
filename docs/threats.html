Threat catalogue#

All threats of both templates. Filter by template, STRIDE category, family or priority, or search any text, including ATT&CK and IEC 62443 references. Click a row to see the description, mitigation and references.

In TMT, the titles name the actual source and target elements of the flow instead of "source" and "target".

IDs#

  • OT- product and system threats, PL- plant and site threats. The letter after the dash is the STRIDE category.
  • IDs ending in R are the data-at-rest threats for a store that is only read from. See How threats are generated.
  • IDs are stable across versions, so you can reference them from requirements, tests and risk registers.
